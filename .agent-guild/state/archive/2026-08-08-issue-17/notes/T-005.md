Writing every note v2 closes the hole by putting my tokens under the lint instead of past it, and it also makes the `RECURRING` line real evidence rather than something I have to assert. I hand-checked tokens against the ten-row table anyway, extracting the way `extract_body` does (body only, above `## Raw Content`, wiki links stripped): the survivors are `[memory candidate: project|client]`, `[open question:]`, `[open question resolved:]`, and one `[decision: two-way]`. The scenario should be gradable by counting flags in the output note: expect one, for the positive slug.

**The arc carries the recurrence, not narration.** No prior note says "we keep asking this" about either slug, because that phrasing in a note is the acknowledgement planted where the rule cannot see it. Instead the resolver drifts (`@unknown` → `@Iris Vogel` → back), an action item rolls over unchanged, and the people who could close either question are never in the room. **Interference.** The transcript is a carrier review — no-show counts, a carrier's dispatch board, the grace window, carrier code duplication. Terminal hours, coverage, and the phase 2 extended windows are absent by construction; I avoided the vocabulary entirely rather than writing around it, and grepped the inbox for staff/hours/shift/evening/overtime/extended/phase 2 to confirm. The positive question's origin lives in a note 1 quote about six o'clock being where the day ends by default, which is in `notes/` where it cannot suppress anything.

**A third slug, `kiosk-print-fallback`, opens in two notes and resolves in the fourth.** It exists so the scope shows questions that do get answered; against that, two questions still open after four sessions reads as an engagement rather than as a token repeated for a test. It stays at two notes, under the threshold, so it cannot compete for the flag.

Records are v2 as well, so their frontmatter key order is under the lint too. No `MEMORY.md`, `INDEX.md`, or `index.md` under `_memory/`.
