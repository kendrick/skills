Wait—that last message did not land. Say it again, like one human talking to another.

With an argument, re-pitch that part in the context of the last response. Bare, re-pitch the whole last response, including what your tool activity concluded—not just the final prose.

Open with one or two sentences of context, then re-pitch in the spirit of ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English:

- Materially shorter than the original. Lead with the one point that matters; support it only as needed.
- Short sentences, one idea each.
- Imperative mood for anything the reader should do.
- One meaning per word, the same word every time, everyday words over jargon.
- Active voice. Name who does what.

Plain human talk is the goal; these rules are the means. When a rule would make a sentence sound robotic, keep the human register and fix the sentence another way.

Work only from what the original said: a concrete example is welcome when it illustrates a claim the original already made; new claims are not. End when the restatement ends.
